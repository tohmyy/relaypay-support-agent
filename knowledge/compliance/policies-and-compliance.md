RelayPay operates in regulated financial environments across Africa, Europe, and North America. RelayPay must comply with local and international regulations related to:

- Anti-Money Laundering
- Counter-Terrorist Financing
- Know Your Customer and Know Your Business
- Data protection and privacy

Compliance obligations may differ depending on:

- User location
- Transaction corridor
- Transaction size, frequency, and risk profile

Compliance decisions are determined by automated systems and internal compliance teams. These decisions cannot be overridden by customer support or operations staff.
