## Product Features Overview

RelayPay is a B2B fintech product used by customer support and operations teams to understand payment, invoicing, payout, and reporting features.

### International Payments

RelayPay enables businesses to send and receive international payments across supported corridors in Africa, Europe, and North America. Payments can be made to vendors, contractors, service providers, and business partners.

Supported payment types include:

- Outbound payouts to external bank accounts
- Incoming transfers from international clients

Payment availability depends on:

- Destination country
- Local banking partners
- Compliance and verification status

RelayPay does not currently support real-time or instant international payments. Processing times vary by corridor and external banking systems.

### Multi-Currency Invoicing

RelayPay allows users to create, issue, and manage invoices in multiple currencies from a single dashboard.

Invoice features include:

- Creating invoices in supported currencies
- Adding line items, taxes, and due dates
- Tracking invoice status: draft, sent, or paid

Invoices help businesses request payment from clients, but RelayPay does not guarantee payment collection. Payment success depends on the payer's location, payment method, and bank processing.

### Payout And Beneficiary Management

RelayPay supports payouts to contractors and vendors through managed beneficiary records.

Users can:

- Add and manage beneficiary details
- Reuse beneficiaries for future payouts
- View payout history at a summary level

Adding or updating beneficiary details may require additional verification. Newly added beneficiaries may experience temporary delays before payouts can be processed.

### Transaction Tracking And Reporting

RelayPay provides basic transaction tracking and reporting tools inside the dashboard.

Users can:

- View transaction status updates
- Access historical transaction summaries
- Download basic reports for reconciliation

Transaction statuses may lag behind real-world bank processing times, especially during peak hours or public holidays.

### Account And Team Access

RelayPay accounts are designed for business use.

Features include:

- Business account profiles
- Team member access with role-based permissions

Specific permissions and roles may vary by account type and compliance status.

### Feature Availability And Limitations

Feature availability may vary based on:

- User location
- Compliance requirements
- Banking partner availability

RelayPay does not support:

- Cryptocurrency payments
- Consumer-to-consumer transfers
- Manual overrides of compliance decisions

Some features may be temporarily unavailable due to maintenance or external dependencies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is RelayPay?

RelayPay is a B2B fintech platform that enables businesses to send and receive international payments, issue invoices in multiple currencies, and manage payouts to vendors and contractors.

### Who Is RelayPay Built For?

RelayPay is designed for startups, SMEs, and growing businesses that operate across borders and need a reliable way to manage international payments.

### Which Regions Does RelayPay Support?

RelayPay supports businesses operating across Africa, Europe, and North America. Availability of specific features may vary by country.

### How Do I Create A RelayPay Account?

Users can create an account by signing up on the RelayPay website and completing the required onboarding steps.

### Why Do I Need To Verify My Identity Or Business?

Identity verification is required to comply with financial regulations and to keep the platform secure for all users.

### How Long Does Verification Take?

Verification timelines vary depending on jurisdiction and document completeness. RelayPay does not guarantee verification completion times.

### How Long Do Payments Take To Process?

Local payouts typically take 1 to 2 business days. International payouts usually take 2 to 5 business days, depending on destination and banking partners.

### Why Is My Payment Delayed?

Delays can occur due to bank processing times, public holidays, compliance reviews, or issues with beneficiary details.

### Can RelayPay Guarantee Payment Timelines?

No. Payment timelines depend on external banking systems and regulatory checks.

### How Does RelayPay Charge Fees?

Fees vary based on transaction type, corridor, and payment method. RelayPay displays applicable fees before a transaction is confirmed.

### Are Exchange Rates Fixed?

No. Exchange rates may fluctuate based on market conditions.

### Can I Create Invoices In Multiple Currencies?

Yes. RelayPay supports invoicing in multiple currencies.

### Does RelayPay Automatically Collect Invoice Payments?

RelayPay provides tools to issue and track invoices, but payment collection depends on the payer's payment method and location.

### Why Is My Account Under Review?

Accounts may be reviewed as part of routine compliance and security processes. Reviews do not necessarily indicate a problem.

### What Should I Do If My Account Is Restricted?

Users should contact RelayPay support through official support channels for assistance.

### How Do I Contact RelayPay Support?

Support can be reached through the support options available in the RelayPay dashboard.

### What Issues Require Human Support?

Account-specific questions, disputes, failed transactions, and compliance-related issues are handled by human support teams.

## Policies And Compliance

RelayPay operates in regulated financial environments across Africa, Europe, and North America. RelayPay must comply with local and international regulations related to:

- Anti-Money Laundering
- Counter-Terrorist Financing
- Know Your Customer and Know Your Business
- Data protection and privacy

Compliance obligations may differ depending on:

- User location
- Transaction corridor
- Transaction size, frequency, and risk profile

Compliance decisions are determined by automated systems and internal compliance teams. These decisions cannot be overridden by customer support or operations staff.

### Identity Verification

All RelayPay customers must complete identity verification before accessing full platform functionality.

Verification requirements may include:

- Business registration documents
- Proof of identity for directors, owners, or authorized signatories
- Proof of address or operating location

Verification timelines vary depending on jurisdiction, document quality, and review workload. Additional documentation may be requested after an account has already been approved.

RelayPay does not guarantee verification completion times. Verification outcomes and requests are determined by compliance requirements, not customer support discretion.

### Transaction Monitoring And Risk Reviews

All transactions processed through RelayPay are subject to continuous monitoring.

Transactions may be:

- Automatically approved
- Delayed for review
- Temporarily held pending additional checks

Common triggers for review include:

- Unusual transaction patterns
- Large transaction amounts
- Changes to beneficiary details
- Activity inconsistent with historical usage

The specific rules and thresholds used for monitoring are confidential. RelayPay does not disclose internal risk logic or review criteria.

### Account Restrictions And Suspensions

RelayPay may apply temporary restrictions or suspensions to accounts when:

- Required verification steps are incomplete
- Compliance or risk reviews are ongoing
- Suspicious or inconsistent activity is detected

Restrictions are precautionary measures and do not imply wrongdoing. In most cases, restrictions are lifted once required reviews or actions are completed.

Details about why a specific account was restricted are not always shareable due to regulatory and security considerations.

### Data Security And Privacy

RelayPay protects customer data and financial information through:

- Least-privilege access to internal systems
- Segmentation of customer data
- Secure storage and transmission of sensitive information

Automated systems and customer-facing tools do not have access to:

- Account balances
- Transaction identifiers unless the user provides them
- Personal identity documents
- Internal risk assessments

RelayPay does not share sensitive account information through automated or voice-based systems.

### Disputes, Refunds, And Cancellations

Disputes, refunds, and cancellations require manual handling by RelayPay's support and operations teams.

These processes may involve:

- Reviewing transaction records
- Coordinating with banking partners
- Compliance checks

RelayPay does not guarantee dispute outcomes or resolution timelines. Refunds and cancellations are evaluated case by case.

### Communications

RelayPay communications must:

- Avoid guarantees or promises
- Reflect policy and compliance limitations accurately
- Avoid speculation or assumptions

RelayPay does not:

- Provide legal, tax, or financial advice
- Disclose internal decision-making logic
- Commit to specific outcomes or timelines
