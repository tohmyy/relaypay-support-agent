# RelayPay Brand Direction

Source document: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1el_ITia1CTuPED_T_8D4LghXiIy65ZuekGelZK1WPAs/edit

## Brand Overview

RelayPay is a B2B fintech product used by founders, finance leads, and operations teams to manage cross-border payments and invoicing.

The product deals with money, compliance, and trust-sensitive workflows.

The interface should feel:

- Professional
- Calm
- Minimal
- Trustworthy

Avoid anything playful, flashy, or experimental.

## Logo Placement

Place the logo:

- At the top-left of the interface, or
- Centered at the top for single-screen flows

Do not animate or over-style the logo.

## Color Palette

Use these colors consistently:

- Primary brand color: deep blue
- Secondary or accent color: teal blue
- Background color: off-white or light grey

Use buttons, active states, and highlights with restraint.

## Typography

Use a clean modern sans-serif font.

Preferred options:

- Inter
- System UI font stack

Avoid decorative fonts, monospace fonts, and large variations in font weight.

## Avoid

- Bright or neon colors
- Gradients
- Emojis
- Chat-heavy visual treatment
- Experimental layouts
- Over-branding

When unsure, make the interface simpler and more neutral.
